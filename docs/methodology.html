02 / METHOD

Capture the flow.
Interpret the signal.

The workflow begins with packet collection and ends with a focused interpretation of protocol usage, host behavior, and unusual communication patterns.

01

Collect traffic

Capture a defined sample of packet data in an authorised lab environment using tshark or Wireshark-style tooling.

02

Summarise protocols

Review protocol distribution, common ports, and host-to-host conversation patterns to see what the network is routinely doing.

03

Identify anomalies

Look for repeated patterns, unusual destinations, suspicious timing, or unexpected protocol usage that deserve attention.

04

Document findings

Record the issues, supporting evidence, and a brief explanation so the final report is clear and defensible.
