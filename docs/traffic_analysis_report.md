## Objective
The purpose of this project was to observe traffic in an authorised lab environment and use the results to identify patterns that could point to suspicious activity or weak network security.

## Method

I captured a short sample of network traffic and reviewed the protocols, ports, and conversations visible in the data. From there, I looked for unusual connections, repeated patterns, and anything that seemed out of place.
